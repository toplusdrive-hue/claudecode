Source Text

¶ 1

Hi, I am Jiho. My grandmother asked me to help her today. She lives alone, so I often visit her. She gave me some cookies when I arrived at her house. I gave a thank-you note to her, and she was very happy. She told me to sit on the sofa and rest for a while. Everything in her house looked very peaceful and warm.

¶ 2

I wanted to do something special for her. I made her a delicious sandwich for lunch. I also bought some fresh flowers for her at the market. I had her taste the sandwich, and she loved it. She made me feel very proud with her bright smile. I let her choose the music for our lunch. How proud I was! To me, helping her is more important than playing games.

¶ 3

While we were eating, I saw her smiling at me. I heard her singing a beautiful song, too. This work helped me become a better person. It also helped me to understand my family more. I want my grandmother to stay healthy for a long time. I will make her happy every weekend. Love and trust make our family strong. I won't forget this wonderful day!

Q1내용 일치 · True or False

Read the sentence and choose True or False.문장을 읽고 내용과 일치하는지 고르세요.

Jiho bought fresh flowers for his grandmother at the market.

① True

Jiho는 할머니를 위해 시장에서 신선한 꽃을 샀다고 했으므로 ①번이 정답입니다. (¶ 2 — I also bought some fresh flowers for her at the market.)

Q2위치 파악 · Location

Where did Jiho buy the flowers for his grandmother?Jiho는 할머니를 위한 꽃을 어디에서 샀나요?

① at the market

Jiho는 시장에서 할머니를 위한 신선한 꽃을 샀다고 했으므로 ①번이 정답입니다.

Q3문맥상 쓰임 · Contextual Usage

What does "alone" mean in the passage?글에서 "alone"은 어떤 의미인가요?

④ without other people

할머니가 혼자 살기 때문에 Jiho가 자주 찾아간다는 문맥이므로 alone은 '다른 사람 없이 혼자'라는 의미입니다. 따라서 ④번이 정답입니다.

Q4빈칸 채우기 · Gap-Fill

Choose the correct word to fill in the blank.빈칸에 들어갈 알맞은 단어를 고르세요.

My grandmother alone.

② lives

주어 My grandmother는 3인칭 단수이고, alone은 부사입니다. 1형식 문장에서 현재시제 동사는 lives가 알맞으므로 ②번이 정답입니다.
